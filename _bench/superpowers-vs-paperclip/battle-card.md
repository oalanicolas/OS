## When to pick

**Pick Superpowers** when one human (or one host session) must *build software* for hours without skipping TDD, review, or the plan.

**Pick Paperclip** when many agents, possibly on different CLIs, must be *employed*: who reports to whom, who spends, who is blocked.

**Pick both** when Paperclip is the company and Superpowers is the requiredSkills pack of every engineer-agent. Do not invert that.
